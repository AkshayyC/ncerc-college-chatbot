Institutional Overview

Nehru College of Engineering and Research Centre

Nehru College of Engineering and Research Centre (NCERC) is a UGC Autonomous engineering college sponsored by the Nehru Group of Institutions. Situated in Nila Gardens, Pampady, Thiruvilwamala, Thrissur, Kerala, NCERC is committed to providing quality technical education and research development.

Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by AICTE, NCERC offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral research programmes with an emphasis on academic rigour and laboratory practice.

Electronics & Communication Engineering
